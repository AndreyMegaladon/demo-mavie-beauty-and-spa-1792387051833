Über Mavie Beauty & Spa

At Mavie Beauty & Spa, we believe self-care is more than a luxury — it is an essential part of your well-being. Located at Toblerstrasse 36 in Zürich, our spa provides a serene sanctuary designed to help you feel relaxed, radiant, and completely confident in your skin. Under the expertise of Priscilla Egger, a certified massage therapist and hair removal specialist, we combine professional care with a warm, welcoming ambiance. Our carefully curated range of treatments is tailored to meet your individual needs, offering everything from advanced permanent hair removal solutions to deeply soothing massages. Whether you are looking to unwind after a long, stressful day with a classic or sports massage, or seeking long-lasting smooth skin, our dedicated specialist ensures every experience is personal, professional, and unforgettable. We pride ourselves on creating a stylish, calm environment where you can truly escape the hustle and bustle of the city. Step into Mavie Beauty & Spa as a hidden gem in Zurich and rediscover your natural glow through expert treatments crafted entirely for your mind, body, and soul.
